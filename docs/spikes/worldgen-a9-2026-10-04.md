Opt-in evaluation `backend/tests/eval/test_worldgen_a9.py` (`make eval-local`): two guided-
creation answers, the §23 artifact slot, and the full 18-stage World Author pipeline for the
brief "Ash Over Calder" (low fantasy, a mining town whose silver seam is failing, two AI

## What failed, run by run, and what changed

| Run | Failure | Fix (principle) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guided creation under-inferred; the artifact used `reveal_information` / `create_entity` with invented params and `{templates}` | Interviewer prompt gives mappings; **item effects have a contract**: item-safe primitives only, the params each reads, low-tier bounds, no placeholders |
| 1 | Premise facts used free text as `subject_ref` / `object_ref` | Unknown fact subjects/objects are **folded into the wording** |
| 2 | Cultures stage named invented knowers | Unknown knowers are **dropped** (knowledge only narrows) |
| 3 | Politics re-emitted every earlier fact (4k tokens) | **Repeats are dropped**; the earlier version stands; task says "only new content" |
| 4, 7 | Politics built every power; factions repeated them | Stage decides the **kind** (polity / faction); a faction stage may **refine** an earlier power by ref |
| 5 | Routes from a place never defined | **Soft references** (routes, territory, allies, participants, goal targets, known names, relationships, via-refs, authors, parents) are dropped with a note; structural ones stay strict |
| 6 | A distant region came back latent | Depth is **clamped** to the stage maximum |
| 8 | NPC skills of 6–7 | NPC numbers are **clamped** to the ruleset scale |
| 10 | The cut-off secrets stage was "repaired" into an NPC speech | **Bug (06b):** the truncation repair instruction was actor-specific; now role-specific. World Author budget 2400 → 4096 |
| 9, 11 | A later stage repeats an earlier place to deepen it | Places may be **deepened** by ref (only deeper); a repeated outline place in a deeper stage is promoted one level |
| 12 | `httpx.ReadTimeout` escaped the provider | Transport errors become `ProviderError`; read timeout **scales with `max_tokens`** |
| 13, 19 | NPC stage overran 4096 tokens; the repair timed out server-side | **Truncation salvage** for list-shaped roles: keep every complete top-level item, never a half-written one |
| 14, 17 | `loc.zone` dotted refs, cross-place adjacency, documents conveying undefined facts | Dotted refs are split; unknown adjacency dropped; unknown conveyed facts dropped |
| 15 | Templates without `item.` | Prefixes normalized; unknown NPC/object templates become generic gear; unknown NPC traits/skills dropped |
| 16 | The NPC stage revised a latent participant | Latent actors may be **revised** before play (noted); imported (established) actors may not |
| 18 | `fact.establish` schedules for facts written only in their patch | The fact is taken from the patch; unresolvable schedules dropped |
| 20 | Companion equipment templates; relationships on 0..10 | Generic gear for any unknown template; 0..10 relationships rescaled |

## The policy that emerged

The validator now distinguishes three kinds of problem:

1. **Structure that defines the world** stays strict and fails the stage: where a person lives,
   where an object is, what a clue's object is, mystery truths, the opening place, the player
   character's legality (after fitting), contradictions of established canon or a locked truth.
2. **Links and quantities** are normalized deterministically and recorded as notes on the stage
   (shown in `GET /generation-runs/{id}`): dangling soft references dropped, numbers clamped or
   rescaled, repeats dropped, depth clamped, latent records refined. None of these widens
   knowledge or invents content.
3. **Length** is handled by salvage + continuation, never by asking again from scratch.

## Itemized stages (09c), run 24 and its resume

The heavy stages now run as an outline call plus one call per item (`app/worldgen/itemized.py`).
The first run stopped at one item; resuming it from the stored progress finished the pipeline.

| Stage | Calls | Seconds | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 plain stages before `opportunities` | 1 each | 16–530 | all accepted first time |
| secrets | 4 | 728 | outline + 2 items + 1 item retry |
| starting_settlement | 8 | 833 | outline + 6 places + 1 retry |
| npcs | 11 | 1736 | outline + 6 sheets + 4 single-sheet retries |
| opportunities (run) | 6 | 1214 | 3 of 4 kept; 1 failed twice: objects at a place with no zones |
| opportunities (resume) | 2 | 261 | only the failed item; no new outline |
| party (resume) | 6 | 810 | whole-party check sent 2 sheets back; the repair round fixed them |
| opening (resume) | 1 | 41 | |

The run took 99 minutes and the resume 19 minutes. Without itemizing, the npcs retries alone
would have meant up to four complete rewrites of six sheets. Item prompts run 5–7k tokens
for the settlement and 15k for NPC sheets, which carry the whole catalog.

What this run found, and the fixes:

- **No-zones placement.** The retry was told an object's place has no zones but not where it
  could go, and it made the same mistake again. The error now lists places that have zones and
  offers `holder_ref`.
- **Negative skills.** Budget fitting trimmed the lowest skill first, so a rank-0 skill went
  to −9. Untrained skills are now dropped first. Out-of-range player skills are fitted, not
  rejected.
- **Party rules per sheet.** The no-secrets, goals and relationship rules ran only on the whole
  party, so a sheet passed alone and failed later. They now run on each sheet. Party
  composition stays a whole-stage check.
- **Day-0 schedules.** The build advances the clock to the opening hour, so a day-0 schedule
  was "in the past". Schedules now fire no earlier than the current clock.
- **Reachability.** The quality check followed only direct routes out of the opening place.
  The regional capital was two hops away, inside the town and then the town's road. The check
  now follows routes and containment transitively.

After these fixes the generated world ("Ash Over Calder") builds, passes the quality checks and
starts.

## Still open

- Guided creation infers well but rarely declares `done`; the 8-answer bound ends it.
- Item flavour often relies on `grant_capability` tags the rules do not yet interpret.
- Zone features arrive as snake_case tags (`angry_miners`); narration copes, a display pass
  could humanise them.
- A full itemized run takes ≈ 2 h on A9 (more, smaller calls); NPC item prompts carry the
  whole catalog (15k tokens). Prompt sizes reach 13–14k tokens in late stages (the catalog of everything so far); the
  context compiler's fitting keeps them inside the profile budget, but a per-stage relevance
  filter would cut cost.
